What we make

Bridal and occasion wear

Lehengas, bridal blouses and reception outfits, built around your fabric or ours, with fittings before the big day.

Designer blouses and maggam work

Hand embroidery, aari work, cut-work and lining done in-house. Bring the saree, we match it.

Sarees and ethnic wear

Ready-to-drape sarees, kurtis and dress materials, with fall, pico and blouse stitching on the same order.

How it works

  1. Share your design on WhatsApp

    Send a photo, a reel or just the occasion. We reply with what is possible and a rough price.

  2. Visit for measurements and fabric

    Measurements are taken at the boutique. Bring your own fabric or pick from ours.

  3. Fitting and collection on the agreed date

    We fix the date before stitching starts and do a trial fitting when the outfit needs one.

  4. Alterations on anything we stitch

    Small adjustments after delivery are part of the service.
